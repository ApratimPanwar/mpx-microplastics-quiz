How the benches work

One model, three views

Each bench imports the same model module that produced the numbers in the design review. The isometric drawing, the charts and the requirement table are all computed from it, so they cannot disagree.

Physics, not curve-fits

Darcy flow through media and cake, solid-body rotation, ε–NTU heat exchange with Dean enhancement, PHREEQC lime chemistry. Literature values (Mott KL, Whatman Herzberg times, Yu et al. 2024) fill the constants.

Assumptions are labelled

Palm contact area, cake resistance, water swirl with vanes, microplastics per litre: where a value is assumed, the bench says so and names the bench test that will replace it.

Detection for every concept uses the caught filter as the sample: Nile red stain, a Carson MP-250 pocket microscope on an iPhone, and a size bar in frame. All water-path parts are metal or glass; no plastic touches the water.
