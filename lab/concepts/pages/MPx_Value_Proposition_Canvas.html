MPx — Value Proposition Canvas

Osterwalder & Pigneur, Value Proposition Design (Strategyzer)
PD-298 Dissertation Project · MDes Programme, IISc
Where the filtration, detection and purification systems create fit with the project's primary customer segment.
Value Proposition
Customer Segment

Products & Services

  • Filtration system — replaceable, non-synthetic media that captures microplastics without shedding new plastic
  • Detection system — optical/fluorescence sensing, ≈10 µm resolution, reports particle count & size-class
  • Purification & assurance layer — validated safety labelling plus a real-time or logged confirmation of what was removed

Gain Creators

  • Visible, real-time reading — the #1 trust signal in our own user research (60%, n=47)
  • Extends the RO/UV system people already trust, rather than replacing it
  • Simple / detailed view toggle — serves data-curious users (40%) and the "just filter it" majority (30%) alike
  • Subscription-friendly cartridge pricing — 92% open to a consumables model

Pain Relievers

  • Closes the gap between what people assume is in their water and what's actually there
  • Independent lab certification + honest "filters microplastics, not a certified purifier" labelling
  • Targets microplastics specifically — a gap today's RO/UV/carbon filters aren't built or marketed for
  • Price anchored to stated willingness to pay (₹1,500–5,000, median ₹3,000)

Gains

  • Confidence their daily drinking water is safe
  • Proof, not just a promise
  • No disruption to an already-established filtration habit
  • A sense of control over an invisible risk

Jobs

  • Drink safe water every day (median 1–3 L, several servings)
  • Choose and maintain a home water-treatment setup
  • Protect their household from contamination
  • Decide whether a new claim (microplastics) is worth acting on

Pains

  • Still uneasy despite already filtering — trust in current filter only 3.1/5
  • No way to see or verify what's actually being removed
  • Uncertain science (WHO: no numeric threshold) makes it hard to know if action is warranted
  • Risk of paying for something that doesn't really work
Primary segment mapped above: urban, RO/UV-owning households already concerned about water safety (MPx stakeholder quiz, n = 47, Aug 2026 — 70% already use RO and/or UV; mean concern for health effects 3.94/5). A distinct underserved/rural segment named in the proposal's business case would need its own canvas — its jobs, pains and gains differ (affordability and access likely outweigh real-time data display).
